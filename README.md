# My 9 Maker

Make a "My 9" grid of books, albums, games, films, or TV shows. Type a title and press Enter to find covers, pick one of nine styles, and save the grid as a PNG.

Cover sources: Open Library (books, with every edition's cover), Apple Music (albums, any country store), MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (albums), Steam box art found through Wikidata (PC games), TVmaze (TV), Wikipedia in 14 languages (everything), or a pasted image link.

Styles: Catalogue card, Night sky, Paperback, Terminal, Record crate, Postcard, Arcade, Handheld, and Clean.

Everything runs in the browser. Your list is saved only in your own browser.

Live: https://williamhackspeare.github.io/my9/
