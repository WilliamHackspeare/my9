This is an example list. Replace the covers and titles with your own, or use Clear all to start from blank slots.

Type a title and press Enter (or use Search) to look up covers. Each category starts with the best source for it, and the source menu has more: Open Library, Apple Music (with a choice of country store), MusicBrainz, Steam, TVmaze, Wikipedia in 14 languages, or a pasted image link. For books, "Other editions" shows more covers. You can also click the box to upload your own image, drop one on a row, or paste one while that row is selected.

Right-click or long-press the image to save it.
