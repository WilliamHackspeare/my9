This is an example list. Replace the covers and titles with your own, or use Clear all to start from blank slots.

Type a title and press Enter (or use Search) to look up covers: books come from Open Library, albums from Apple Music, and games, films, and everything else from Wikipedia. For books, "Other editions" shows more covers. You can also click the box to upload your own image, drop one on a row, or paste one while that row is selected.

Right-click or long-press the image to save it.
